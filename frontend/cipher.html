Gujarat Police Sentinel - Officer Manual & Overview

1. What is the Purpose of Sentinel?

Sentinel solves the Gujarat Police Hackathon problem statement by unifying CCTV camera feeds across 26 separate departments into a single, real-time command dashboard. It enables automated ANPR detection, watchlist alerts, and cross-camera route tracing.

2. What Does Each Tab Do?

  • Tactical GIS Map: Browse camera locations across Gujarat, filter by city or department, and inspect live streams directly on the map.
  • Multi-Camera Live Wall: Watch all 30 live camera feeds simultaneously or choose custom matrix grids (4, 9, 16) with in-place camera dropdown switching.
  • Watchlist & Suspect Alerts: Manage flagged suspect vehicles with full Create, Edit, and Delete options, and dispatch police patrols.
  • Vehicle Route Reconstruction: Enter any vehicle registration number to correlate camera detections chronologically and draw its travel path on the GIS map.
  • Registry & Gap Analysis (Model 1): Audits multi-departmental integration coverage and identifies surveillance blind spots across the state.
