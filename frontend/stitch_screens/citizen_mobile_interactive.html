Public Safety & Surveillance Charter

Gujarat Police operates under stringent data governance protocols. Camera feeds are processed exclusively for traffic regulation, crime interdiction, and immediate distress response. No biometric identity matches are retained beyond statutory periods without judicial warrant.

Once a call hits 112, our CAD (Computer-Aided Dispatch) automatically routes the nearest patrol vehicle via live GPS telemetry. Average arrival time in Ahmedabad, Surat, and Vadodara metros is under 7.8 minutes.

Reporting a financial fraud within 2 hours triggers an automated API stop-payment request through the NPCI and Reserve Bank switch, locking funds before they traverse out through money-mule accounts.

Citizen requests are correlated with the nationwide CCTNS database and local police station registers. An SMS receipt with a verified QR certificate is issued directly to the applicant's mobile.
