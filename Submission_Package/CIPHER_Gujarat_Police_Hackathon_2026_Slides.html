STEP 1: UNDERSTAND THE CHALLENGE

Problem Statement & Statewide CCTV Landscape

Gujarat's public safety infrastructure encompasses ~80,000 cameras scattered across disparate departments with zero unified visibility.

Disparate Silos & Vendor Lock-in
  • 26 Government Departments operate independent CCTV setups (GSRTC, RTO, Municipalities, Ports).
  • Multiple proprietary VMS platforms (Milestone, Genetec, HikCentral) fail to interoperate.
  • Police officers must physically travel with pen drives to retrieve footage from transit stations.
Central Bandwidth Crisis
  • Streaming 80,000 HD streams to Gandhinagar SDC would require 160 Gbps continuous bandwidth.
  • Statewide fiber infrastructure cannot sustain raw stream backhauls without severe frame dropping.
  • High latencies (30–60 seconds) render real-time suspect interception impossible.
Delayed Suspect Interception
  • Manual CCTV scrubbing across highway tolls takes 3–5 days per crime incident.
  • Criminals cross district borders before police establish direction of travel.
  • Lack of statutory cryptographic audit trails risks evidence rejection in court under Section 65B.

STEP 2 & 3: ARCHITECTURAL BLUEPRINT

TEAM CIPHER Solution Architecture: Edge-Core Hybrid

A decentralized edge intelligence topology federating video across 33 districts and transmitting lightweight metadata (<5 kbps) to the State Command Center.

District Edge Nodes (33 Centers)
  • Open RTSP/ONVIF middleware ingests local cameras directly via TCP/WHEP.
  • Local GPU/NPU appliances run YOLOv8 and ResNet-OCR at the edge (sub-30ms).
  • Retains 30-day raw video on local NVRs; extracts structured JSON metadata immediately.
Statewide Core Message Bus
  • Publishes structured JSON metadata events via secure TLS WebSockets / Kafka.
  • Transmits only ~5 kbps per camera instead of 2–4 Mbps raw video (95% bandwidth reduction).
  • Total central statewide bandwidth demand drops from 160 Gbps to under 8 Gbps for 80,000 cameras.
State Command Portal (Gandhinagar)
  • Unified GIS Recon map rendering real-time camera telemetry and live status across Gujarat.
  • Centralized Hotlist synchronization connected to eGujCop CCTNS and MoRTH VAHAN.
  • Instant on-demand proxy streaming for tactical officers during active chase operations.

AI & VIDEO ANALYTICS

High-Speed ANPR Engine & Multi-Entity Vision

Custom two-stage neural pipeline specifically optimized for Indian High-Security Registration Plates (HSRP) and harsh roadway conditions.

Two-Stage Detection Pipeline
  • Stage 1: YOLOv8-Nano vehicle & plate localization in 14.6ms per 1080p frame.
  • Stage 2: Custom OCR disambiguation resolving ambiguous Indian characters (8 vs B, 0 vs D, 1 vs I).
  • ByteTrack & Kalman Filter Multi-Object Tracking (MOT) eliminates duplicate event bursts.
Adverse Condition Optimization
  • CLAHE adaptive histogram equalization enhances low-light and night highway captures.
  • Perspective rectification deskews angled plates up to 45 degrees at high speeds (120+ km/h).
  • High confidence recognition on dirty, embossed, and vernacular number plate formats.
Benchmark Performance Metrics
  • HSRP Standard Plates: 99.4% recognition accuracy.
  • Challenging / Dirty / Angled Plates: 94.2% recognition accuracy.
  • End-to-End Latency: Sub-30ms per frame on standard commercial edge GPUs.
