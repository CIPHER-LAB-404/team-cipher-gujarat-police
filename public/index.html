Public Knowledgebase

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on vehicle theft intimation, cyber fraud golden hour protocol, and emergency response rights.

Once you submit your vehicle registration plate and details through this portal, our automated backend immediately flags the plate in the central ANPR CCTV matrix across all 33 districts. The moment the suspect vehicle passes any highway or municipal camera checkpoint, an audio-visual alert with live GPS coordinates is dispatched to the nearest police control room.

The "Golden Hour" refers to the critical first 2 to 4 hours immediately following an unauthorized online banking, UPI, or credit card transaction. Calling 1930 immediately enables the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal to trigger automated API liens with commercial banks and payment gateways to freeze funds before they are withdrawn by fraudsters.

SHE Teams operate across all cities and rural talukas in Gujarat. They are specialized units led by women police officers trained to handle harassment, stalking, eve-teasing, and domestic distress. Women traveling alone late at night can also contact 1091 to request patrol vehicle accompaniment or live GPS tracking until they reach home safely.

FINANCIAL CYBER FRAUD PROTOCOL

Dial 1930 — National Cyber Crime Helpline

Dial 1930 within the "Golden Hour" (first 2-4 hours) after an unauthorized banking or UPI transaction to immediately lien and freeze fraudulent accounts before funds can be siphoned.

The Golden Hour Direct Bank Interception

Real-time API integration with 150+ Indian banks, payment gateways, and UPI platforms to halt cash withdrawals.

What to Keep Ready When Calling 1930:

1. Transaction UTR / Reference Number & Date/Time
2. Beneficiary Account / UPI ID / Phone Number
3. Bank Account Number & Debit Card Last 4 Digits
